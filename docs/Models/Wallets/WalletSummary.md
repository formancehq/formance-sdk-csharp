| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| `AvailableFunds`                                                                                   | Dictionary<String, *BigInteger*>                                                                   | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                 | Funds available to debit, keyed by asset. Covers balances with no expiry and those not yet expired |
| `ExpiredFunds`                                                                                     | Dictionary<String, *BigInteger*>                                                                   | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                 | Funds held in balances whose expiry date has passed, keyed by asset                                |
| `ExpirableFunds`                                                                                   | Dictionary<String, *BigInteger*>                                                                   | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                 | Funds held in balances carrying an expiry date that has not yet passed, keyed by asset             |
| `HoldFunds`                                                                                        | Dictionary<String, *BigInteger*>                                                                   | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                 | Funds currently locked by pending holds, keyed by asset                                            |
